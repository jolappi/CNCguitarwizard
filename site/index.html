What this page does

It runs the real cncguitarwizard Python package in your browser (via Pyodide) and builds the complete Prototype001 package: the FreeCAD script, the body G-code for a GRBL machine, toolpath plots and the build report — all without a server.

The .FCStd and STEP models need FreeCAD itself: download Prototype001_freecad.py and run it with freecadcmd or as a macro, or Save design and build everything locally with python -m cncguitarwizard build-prototype001 --design my_guitar.json.

Every value below is a field of Prototype001Parameters or MachiningParameters. Hover a field's name to see what it means. Changed values are highlighted. Lists are edited as JSON. Rarely needed fields sit behind each group's Advanced fold.

Your design

Drag the round handles to shape the body. Click the outline to add a handle there; Alt/Option-click or right-click a handle to remove it. A template chosen in Start from is loaded at once (Load loads it again) — Design by Jone, or a Les Paul, Stratocaster, Jackson RR, Alexi Hexed, Mockingbird, Telecaster, SG, Explorer, Flying V or Jazz Bass style mockup — with its own cavity placements; what the template before it set and you have not changed since goes back to its default. Drag the control cavity (its pots come along), a single pot, the switch cavity, the battery box, a neck bolt (dashed, drilled from the back) or the jack to move it; Shift-drag the control cavity, the battery box, the jack or a pickup to turn it (round ones only move); drag one of the control cavity's square end handles to stretch or shrink it and its cover from that end, or a side handle to make them wider or narrower; the pickup routes slide along the neck. With a pickguard on, its square handles shape it: click its edge to add one, Alt/Option-click or right-click one to remove it, drag its edge to move the whole guard or onto Create NC file for its own programs (Auto pickguard goes back to the automatic guard). With an arm contour on (body_arm_contour_depth), the green line with round handles is where it starts: drag a handle, click the line to add one, Alt/Option-click or right-click one to remove it; the line's ends sit on the body's edge (Auto arm contour goes back to the automatic one); with a belly cut on (body_belly_cut_depth) its line on the back is drawn the same way in blue (Auto belly cut). With a stepped top on (body_stepped_top), each step's line is drawn in purple with diamond handles, straight between them: drag a handle, click a line to add one, Alt/Option-click or right-click one to remove it (Auto steps goes back to the steps body_top_step_insets in from the edge). With body_engraving on, the decorative engraving is drawn in blue lines; New pattern lays it out from a new random seed (body_engraving_seed). Drag a feature right off the body to remove it (you are asked first), or onto Create NC file to cut it on its own. The neck, its pocket and the bridge follow the neck and scale and stay put. Undo and Redo (Ctrl/Cmd+Z, Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z) step back and forward through the design's changes, whichever editor or field they were made in. To draw the outline in another program, Export SVG saves it as a template, 1:1 in millimetres: edit the black path in its Outline layer (or draw a new closed one there) round the locked Reference layer's neck, routes and cavities, keep the three red registration marks, and Import SVG reads it back as handles on the drawn line (within 0.25 mm of it; Undo takes it back). The grid is 50 mm.

    Headstock design

    Drag the handles to shape the two edges; drag a tip handle to change the length. Click an edge to add a handle there, or the tip to shape it with one (drag it out for a round nose, in for a notch); Alt/Option-click or right-click a handle to remove it. For a sharp point (Jackson style) drag one tip corner onto the other: within 3 mm they meet, and the point then moves as one (Shift-drag a corner to part them again). The tuner holes come from the headstock style and stay put; every hole must keep its distance from the edge. Until you move a handle the outline follows the fitted one (a changed style redraws it); Start over goes back to it; Undo steps back a change, Redo forward again. A neck chosen in Start from is loaded whole (Load loads it again), its tuners' places with it: the Telecaster neck sets a slotted nut (the fretboard running on under it), a flat six-in-line headstock drawn as a Telecaster's and a truss rod adjusted at the heel (set truss_rod_adjustment to headstock for the modern way, reached behind the nut; truss_rod_spoke_wheel says whether the adjuster is a spoke wheel). Lettering typed in headstock_engraving_text is drawn in blue: drag it to move it (it must keep 2 mm from the edge, the nut's seat, the tuner holes and the truss-rod cover). With the truss rod adjusted at the headstock (truss_rod_adjustment) its cover is drawn over the dashed trough, in the style chosen (truss_rod_cover_style: a Gibson bell, Ibanez / ESP, a PRS teardrop or a rounded rectangle): drag one of its square corners to draw your own cover (click its edge to add a corner, Alt/Option-click or right-click one to remove it), or a round handle past its far end or beside its widest side to make it longer or wider; choosing a style again goes back to it. It must cover the trough and keep its screws in wood and clear of the edges and the tuner holes. Export SVG saves the outline as a template, 1:1 in millimetres, to edit in another program: the black path in its Outline layer runs from the nut on one side round the tip to the nut on the other, round the locked Reference layer's nut, tuner holes (dashed, the edge's clearance round them) and truss-rod cover; keep the three red registration marks, and Import SVG reads it back as edges and tip handles (each edge running away from the nut, the tip across from one edge's end to the other's). The grid is 10 mm.

    Inlay design

    The first fret marker's space on the board, the nut to the left: drag a corner to shape the marker, click a side to add a corner, Alt/Option-click or right-click one to remove it. Every other marker is the same shape fitted to its own fret space and the board's width there, as the whole board below shows. Editing any style's marker starts a drawn one (inlay_style custom, its corners in inlay_points); it keeps 1 mm from the fret slots and the board's edges, and its corners are rounded 1 mm. Start over goes back to a block; Undo steps back a change, Redo forward again. The grid is 5 mm.
