Your design

Drag the round handles to shape the body. Double-click the outline to add a handle there; Alt/Option-click or right-click a handle to remove it. Start from loads a template — Design by Jone, or a Les Paul, Stratocaster, Jackson RR or Jazz Bass style mockup — with its own cavity placements. Drag the control cavity (its pots come along), a single pot, the switch cavity, the battery box, a neck bolt (dashed, drilled from the back) or the jack to move it; the pickup routes slide along the neck. The neck, its pocket and the bridge follow the neck and scale and stay put. The grid is 50 mm.

Headstock design

Drag the handles to shape the two edges; drag a tip handle to change the length. Double-click an edge to add a handle there; Alt/Option-click or right-click a handle to remove it. The tuner holes come from the headstock style and stay put; every hole must keep its distance from the edge. The grid is 10 mm.

What this page does

It runs the real cncguitarwizard Python package in your browser (via Pyodide) and builds the complete Prototype001 package: the FreeCAD script, the body G-code for a GRBL machine, toolpath plots and the build report — all without a server.

The .FCStd and STEP models need FreeCAD itself: download Prototype001_freecad.py and run it with freecadcmd or as a macro.
